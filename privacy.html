PDF Toolbox – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

PDF Toolbox processes all PDF files and images locally on your device. Your files are never uploaded to any server.

We do not collect, store or share any personal data. We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising. Your signature is kept only in memory while the app is open.

The app downloads its open-source PDF libraries (pdf-lib, PDF.js, JSZip) from the jsDelivr CDN the first time it runs and then keeps them on your device for offline use.
